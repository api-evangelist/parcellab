As of June 19, 2026, EU retailers must provide a clearly labeled electronic withdrawal button for the 14-day cooling-off window. parcelLab argues the real challenge is the operational infrastructure behind it: order identification, fulfillment checks, and return logistics, and shows how its returns portal supports withdrawal reasons, confirmations, labels, and workflow integration.
